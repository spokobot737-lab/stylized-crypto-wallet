# Cryptocurrency artwork

- sol.svg: official Solana gradient logomark, downloaded unchanged from https://solana.com/src/img/branding/solanaLogoMark.svg (guidelines: https://solana.com/branding).
- eth.svg and btc.svg: canonical Ethereum diamond and orange Bitcoin mark from cryptocurrency-icons 0.18.1, https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons. See the accompanying license (CC0).

These icons identify assets and networks. They do not imply sponsorship or affiliation. Artwork is bundled locally; themes never rotate, recolor or replace the cryptocurrency marks with font glyphs.
